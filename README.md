## Basic Employee Sorting Program
5. Okoro and sons company recently advertised vacancies.
The number of applicants are much and have to be pruned down while the successfull applicants are to be fixed in the following departments: Engineering, Admin, Customer Care and Security based on certain criteria. The company has a policy of not employing people that are not up to 18 years and people above 50 years. Any man not up to 25 years old is placed in Customer care department. Any woman below 31 years is also placed in the Customer care department. Any man below 45 years old is posted to the Engineering department. The rest of the women are posted to the Admin department. The rest of the men are recruited as security men. write a code to place all the personnel in their various departments accordingly immediately they make their application(if they are eligible).
